About Jack
======
Jack is a third-year PhD student focused on infectious disease epidemiology and antimicrobial resistance (AMR) trends, leveraging extensive electronic health records (EHRs) from Hong Kong.

Research interests include dementia pharmacoepidemiology, using methods such as self-controlled case series and target trial emulation.

He is actively seeking post-graduation opportunities outside academia and is interested in translating research using real-world data into practical health insights.

He dreams of trekking or biking after graduation and enjoys running analyses and writing papers in the mountains.
